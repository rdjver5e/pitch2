Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Where is Elegance By Rhea?

Elegance By Rhea is a jewellery showroom in Kolkata. You can open the exact location in Google Maps from the Visit section on this page.

What jewellery do you sell?

We sell rings, earrings, necklaces and bangles for daily wear, gifts, festivals and weddings.

Can I see prices before I visit?

Yes. Send us a WhatsApp message with the piece you like. We reply with the price and whether it is available.

Can I try the jewellery on?

Yes. Visit the showroom and we will bring out the pieces you chose. You can also book a time on WhatsApp so they are ready.

Can I exchange my old jewellery?

Please ask us on WhatsApp. We will explain how exchange works for the piece you choose.

Can I ask for a custom design?

Send us a photo or an idea on WhatsApp, and we will tell you what is possible.
